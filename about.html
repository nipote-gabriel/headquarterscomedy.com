The HQ Universe

We make funny things.

Headquarters Comedy is a full-service manufacturer of humor, based in New York City.

We started out producing pop-up standup shows, and learned that humor is a very powerful tool.

In addition to our own live shows and content, Headquarters helps people & brands be funnier and more memorable, whether it's for a speech, a keynote, or just day-to-day.

Some other projects from the HQ Universe below.

WorldCorp International

WorldCorp International is a global think-tank, market research, and strategy firm. We exist to push the frontier of research into how to make the world more sterile, boring, joyless, and corporate.

FastNews Network

Breaking news, breaking constantly. We get you the news so fast, we're not sure if it's accurate. All of our headlines are satirical, but somehow it feels like you learn more from reading our news than the real news. Fake news, real insight.

The Gary Luna Show

An experimental, animated late night talk show featuring cameos from celebrity guests, discussing culture and current events.

Backstory

Headquarters Comedy was started by Gabe Nipote, a comedian & entrepreneur based in New York City.

Prior to Headquarters, Gabe had a software company called Halla, which was acquired by Instacart in 2025. Now, you can find Gabe as a regular at The Buddha Room, or performing standup at other venues all over New York City, such as New York Comedy Club, The Stand, Flop House, and more.
