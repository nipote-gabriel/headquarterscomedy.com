01
The Big Change

Attention doesn't work the way it used to — and nobody sent your comms team the memo.

Audiences were raised on TikTok, Shorts, and unscripted street interviews. The feed rewards a real human moment over a polished slide, every time. A press release goes straight into the void. A keynote read off a deck gets clipped, captioned, and roasted before the Q&A even starts. Meanwhile every employee is a broadcaster with a phone in their pocket, whether the company signed off on it or not.

Winners & Losers

This change is already splitting the field.

Same market, same budget, wildly different outcomes — depending on whether you sound like a human being.

Winning
  • Leaders who sound like humans on stage, on camera, and in a Slack AMA
  • Brands that show up in the feed, not just the press release
  • Teams that turn one good interview into three weeks of content
Losing
  • Executives who read the slide out loud, word for word
  • Comms teams shipping content nobody opens, let alone shares
  • Brands that sound like they were written by their own legal department
