HQ Universe

Headquarters Comedy is an industrial manufacturer of humor, based in New York City.

In addition to our own live shows and content, Headquarters helps people & brands bring humor and memorability into their speeches, presentations, content, and events.

Projects

  • WorldCorp International WorldCorp International is a full-service global think tank and consultancy serving Fortune 500 companies, mid-market disruptors, and pre-idea startups. We make you funny, interesting, and memorable. ↗
  • Panic News Network Breaking news, breaking constantly. We get you the news so fast, we're not sure if it's accurate. All of our headlines are satirical, but somehow it feels like you learn more from reading our news than the real news. Fake news, real insight. ↗
  • The Gary Luna Show An experimental, animated late night talk show featuring cameos from celebrity guests, discussing culture and current events. ↗

Backstory

Headquarters Comedy was started by Gabe Nipote, a comedian & entrepreneur based in New York City.

Prior to Headquarters, Gabe had a software company called Halla, which was acquired by Instacart in 2025. Now, you can find Gabe as a regular at The Buddha Room, or performing standup at other venues all over New York City, such as New York Comedy Club, The Stand, Flop House, and more.

What We Offer

Events people want to attend.

Shows for Teams

Corporate shows suck. We've re-designed them for startups. Perfect for teams up to 100, with good culture. Our shows leave your team more tightly knit and help blow off steam so you can get back to your 9-9-6.

Shows for Brands

Sponsor one of our public shows and get your brand in front of a smart, engaged audience that actually shows up to things. Tasteful, effective, not embarrassing. We capture valuable content of people engaging with your brand in real life.

Private Shows

Birthday? Launch event? Fundraiser? Anniversary? Whatever you need, we can put it together. We'll source the venue, book the talent, capture content, and leave your audience laughing. We can roast, keep it clean, or do anything in between.

The Process

How it works.

  1. 1

    Tell Us What You Need

    Send us an email with your event goals, audience, date range, and budget. The more detail the better.

  2. 2

    We Put Together a Proposal

    We'll come back with a format recommendation, lineup concept, and pricing. Usually within a few days.

  3. 3

    We Handle the Rest

    Once we're aligned, we take it from there. You show up, enjoy the show, take the credit.
