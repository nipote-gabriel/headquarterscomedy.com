What We Offer

Events people want to attend.

Shows for Teams

Corporate shows suck. We've re-designed them for startups. Perfect for teams up to 100, with good culture. Our shows leave your team more tightly knit and help blow off steam so you can get back to your 9-9-6.

Shows for Brands

Sponsor one of our public shows and get your brand in front of a smart, engaged audience that actually shows up to things. Tasteful, effective, not embarrassing. We capture valuable content of people engaging with your brand in real life.

Private Shows

Birthday? Launch event? Fundraiser? Anniversary? Whatever you need, we can put it together. We'll source the venue, book the talent, capture content, and leave your audience laughing. We can roast, keep it clean, or do anything in between.

The Process

How it works.

1

Tell Us What You Need

Send us an email with your event goals, audience, date range, and budget. The more detail the better.

2

We Put Together a Proposal

We'll come back with a format recommendation, lineup concept, and pricing. Usually within a few days.

3

We Handle the Rest

Once we're aligned, we take it from there. You show up, enjoy the show, take the credit.
